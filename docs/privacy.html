Privacy policy

InkDay · last updated October 25, 2026

InkDay is an application that runs on your PocketBook e-reader. It has no server, no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising. This policy explains what data InkDay uses, where it is stored and who it is sent to.

Google user data

If you connect a Google account in InkDay settings, InkDay requests read-only access to Google Calendar (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly). With this access InkDay reads:

  • the list of your calendars: identifier and name;
  • events of the calendars you select, for the next 1–7 days: title, location, start and end time.

This data is used only to display your calendars in InkDay settings and your events on the InkDay dashboard of your own e-reader. InkDay does not create, change or delete anything in your calendar.

Storage. The calendar list, the displayed events and the OAuth refresh token are stored only on your e-reader, in the /mnt/ext1/system/config/ folder. They are not sent to the developer or to any third party. The data is replaced at each update; events outside the displayed period are not kept.

Sharing. Google user data is not shared, sold or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising or to train AI or machine learning models. Requests go directly from the e-reader to Google over HTTPS.

Sign-in page. During sign-in, Google redirects your browser to the InkDay sign-in page on GitHub Pages (kif-fando.github.io). This static page has no server-side code: it saves the one-time authorization code as a file in the e-reader's Downloads folder, where InkDay picks it up and deletes it. The code cannot be used without a secret key that is generated on the e-reader and never leaves it. GitHub, as the host of the page, may log technical request data such as the IP address according to the GitHub privacy statement.

Deletion. Press Disconnect in InkDay settings to delete the token and all calendar data from the e-reader. You can also revoke InkDay's access at any time in your Google Account permissions. Deleting InkDay's files from the e-reader removes all data as well.

InkDay's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services

  • Weather — the coordinates of the city you select are sent to Open-Meteo to get the forecast.
  • City search — the text you type is sent to the Open-Meteo Geocoding API.
  • Location by IP — on first run, or when you press "Detect by IP", the e-reader's public IP address is sent to ipwho.is to find an approximate city.

The selected city and the last weather result are stored only on your e-reader.

Children

InkDay is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the source repository. Questions and requests: GitHub issues.
